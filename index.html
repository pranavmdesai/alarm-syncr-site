Alarm Syncr by Kheloton LLC

Your phone’s alarms, on your wrist.

Alarm Syncr is an app for Android phones and Wear OS watches. Set alarms on your phone and choose whether each one rings on your phone, your watch, or both. It can also mirror your Samsung Clock or Google Clock alarm to your watch.
